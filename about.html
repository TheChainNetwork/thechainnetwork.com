Why Chain Network exists

Chain Network is clear, honest education for anyone stepping into crypto and digital finance. No hype. No jargon left unexplained.

Through simple, classroom-style guides we explain how blockchains work, how to store and protect your crypto, how to avoid the costly mistakes beginners make, and what the rules mean for individuals and businesses.

The mission is to educate and protect newcomers. The single most common way new users lose money is sending funds on the wrong network, so the early guides focus hard on the calm, practical habits that prevent it.

The course is built and presented by Mike Curgenven, a UK-based educator, and is produced in English, Spanish, Hindi and Portuguese.

This is education and awareness only. It is not financial advice at any point.
